# Thulo Bazaar — Support Assistant Knowledge

## What Thulo Bazaar is
Nepal's online classifieds marketplace (thulobazaar.com.np). People buy and sell locally: mobiles, vehicles, property, furniture, electronics, jobs, services and more. Posting ads is FREE. Available as a website and an Android/iOS app ("Thulo Bazaar" on Play Store and App Store).

## Posting an ad
- Tap "Post Ad", add photos first — the AI can suggest a title, category, description and an editable price estimate from the photos.
- Required: title, category, price, and a location at municipality level or deeper (province/district alone is not enough).
- Ads are reviewed before going live. Many are approved automatically within minutes; some are held for the human team to check. Held/pending ads show in Dashboard → Pending with the reason.
- If an ad stays pending, the team reviews it as soon as possible — reposting the same ad does NOT speed it up and duplicate titles are blocked.
- Rejected ads show the rejection reason in the dashboard; fix the issue and edit the ad to resubmit it.

## Editing ads
- Pending ads can be edited freely; edits are reviewed again.
- LIVE ads can be edited up to 3 times per month. Edits are checked again (photos and title changes included) — for most sellers the ad returns to review, while verified businesses stay live and are checked right after.

## Verification
- Individual verification: submit an ID document (citizenship, license, passport) from the dashboard. Gives a verified badge and more trust.
- Business verification: submit business documents. Verified businesses get a shop page, a badge, and their ads publish immediately (checked right after publishing).
- Approval is done by the human team; the assistant cannot approve, reject, or check the progress of a verification.

## Shops
- Verified businesses get a public shop page listing all their ads; a custom shop link can be set in profile settings.

## Promotions (paid)
- Ads can be promoted as Featured, Urgent, or Sticky for more visibility. Payment is via Khalti or eSewa inside the app/site (prices in NPR, shown on the promotion screen).
- The assistant cannot see payments or fix payment problems — payment issues always go to the human team.

## Refund policy (from the Terms of Service)
- Ad promotions: no cash refunds. If there is a payment or display issue, the team re-applies the promotion instead.
- Verification fees: non-refundable, even if the application is rejected — but resubmission is free.
- All sales are final unless there is a proven technical error, in which case the service is re-provided. Any refund request still goes to the human team.

## Account deletion
- Profile Settings → Security → Delete Account; confirmed with an OTP to the registered phone.
- The account then enters a 30-day soft-deletion period (deactivated, data preserved). Logging in within 30 days automatically reactivates it and cancels the deletion.
- After 30 days everything is permanently deleted (ads, images, messages, documents, payment history) — irreversible.

## Verification validity
- Paid verification lasts for the chosen duration (e.g. 30/90/180/365 days); after expiry, re-verify and pay again.

## Chat & contacts
- Buyers contact sellers with in-app chat or the phone number on the ad. You can report or block a user from the chat or their profile.

## Account
- Log in with phone number (OTP code by SMS) or email and password. "Forgot password" on the login screen resets it.
- Phone number or email changes, account deletion, and login problems the steps above don't fix must go to the human team.

## Safety rules (share these when relevant)
- Meet in person in a public place; inspect the item before paying.
- Never send advance payments, deposits, or "delivery fees" to strangers.
- Do not share OTP codes or passwords with anyone — Thulo Bazaar staff never ask for them.
- Report suspicious ads with the Report button; the team reviews every report.

## What the assistant must escalate to the human team
- Anything requiring account lookups or changes: payments and refunds, verification status, unlocking accounts, changing phone/email, deleting accounts or ads that will not delete.
- Disagreement with a moderation, rejection, or verification decision.
- Reports of fraud, scams, harassment, or safety concerns.
- Bugs the steps here do not fix, or anything not covered above.
