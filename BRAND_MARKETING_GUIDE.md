## 1. What Is Thulo Bazaar?

**Thulo Bazaar is Nepal's leading online classifieds marketplace** — a platform where people across Nepal can **buy, sell, and rent** almost anything: mobiles, electronics, vehicles, property, jobs, services, and more.

- **Website:** https://thulobazaar.com.np
- **Mobile app:** Android & iOS (`com.thulobazaar.mobile`)
- **Based in:** Kathmandu, Nepal
- **Coverage:** Nationwide (all provinces, districts, municipalities)
- **Core promise:** Posting ads is **100% FREE**; premium features are optional.

**One-line description:**
> Nepal's leading classifieds marketplace. Buy, sell, and rent across Nepal with ease.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Friendly, local, trustworthy.** We're Nepal's own marketplace — not a foreign import.
- **Simple & clear.** Plain language, no jargon. Buying/selling should feel easy.
- **Light Nepali flavor welcome.** English with Nepali phrases ("Nenglish") works well for urban audiences; full Devanagari नेपाली for broader reach.
- **Emphasize:** FREE, trust/safety, nationwide reach, convenience.
- **Avoid:** Overpromising, fake urgency, anything that undermines the "safe & trusted" positioning.

## 5. Key Features (what we offer)

### Core marketplace
- ✅ **Free ad posting** — zero cost, no commission, no hidden fees
- ✅ **Search & filters** — by location (province/district/area), category, price range, condition (Brand New / Like New / Used)
- ✅ **Latest ads** feed + **Featured ads** carousel
- ✅ **Favorites/wishlist** (heart icon)
- ✅ **Ad view tracking & analytics** for sellers
- ✅ **Negotiable price** indicator

### Trust & verification
- 🔵 **Verified Individual badge** (blue tick) — phone/ID verified
- 🟡 **Verified Business badge** (golden) — registered businesses w/ license
- Verification benefits: golden badge, custom shop URL, better search ranking, more photo uploads (up to 10), business showcase, **up to 3x increased buyer confidence**

### Shops / business profiles
- Custom shop URL, cover photo, logo/avatar, description
- Contact links (WhatsApp, website, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps)
- All active ads shown, total views, "member since" date

### Communication
- 💬 **Real-time in-app messaging** (Socket.io) between buyers & sellers
- 📞 Phone number display + "Contact Seller" button on every ad

### Promotions (paid upgrades — sell faster)
- ⭐ **Featured** — top of search results with highlight
- ⏰ **Urgent** — priority placement + time-sensitive badge
- 📌 **Sticky** — keeps ad at top of category for a set duration
- Activate instantly after payment; promo-code campaigns supported

### Payments
- **Khalti, eSewa, bank transfers** — all popular Nepali methods. Currency: **NPR**

### Mobile app (Flutter)
- Android & iOS, native camera & gallery upload, optional location
- 🔔 **Push notifications** (Firebase FCM) for messages/offers
- Google OAuth login, dark mode (Material 3)

### Safety & moderation
- Safety tips on every ad (meet in public, inspect before paying, never pay in advance, beware unrealistic offers)
- Automated profanity filter + manual ad review + user reporting
- Account suspension for violations
- 🔐 **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** (TOTP)

### Support & content
- Help Center & FAQ, support tickets
- Blog system (SEO/content articles by category)

## 11. Things to Get Right (accuracy checklist)

- [ ] Posting is **free** — never imply listing costs money (only promotions/verification are paid).
- [ ] Domain is **thulobazaar.com.np** (NOT .com).
- [ ] Two badge types: **blue (individual)**, **gold (business)** — don't mix them up.
- [ ] Promotions are **Featured / Urgent / Sticky** — use these exact names.
- [ ] Payments: **Khalti, eSewa, bank transfer**.
- [ ] App is on **both Android & iOS**.
- [ ] Coverage is **all of Nepal**, not just Kathmandu.
